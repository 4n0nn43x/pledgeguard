A registry no single institution controls

The registry is a Canton decentralized party run jointly by three members. Day-to-day checks are automatic. Any sensitive decision needs the approval of two members out of three, and the ledger itself enforces that rule. Proven on a three-node Canton network; on this DevNet deployment the registry runs as a single party with the same automation.

One approval is not enough

When a single member tries to act alone, the ledger rejects the decision. The two-of-three rule is written into the registry's contract on Canton, so no server, operator or member can bypass it.
